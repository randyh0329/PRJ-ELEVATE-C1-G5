# Global Enterprise Leave Policy (2026 Edition)
Document ID: POL-HR-LEAVE-2026
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 3.2
Owner: Global People Operations

## 1. Annual Vacation Leave
All full-time employees accrue paid vacation hours each bi-weekly pay period.
- Standard accrual: 15 to 20 days annually based on tenure.
- Maximum carry-over into the subsequent calendar year is 40 accrued hours.
- Requests must be submitted via WorkWeek at least 2 business days in advance.
- All vacation requests require formal manager sign-off before being finalized.

## 2. Sick Leave
- Full-time employees accrue 8 hours of sick leave per full month of service (up to 12 days per calendar year).
- Sick leave may be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or care of an immediate family member.
- Absences exceeding 3 consecutive business days require medical certification.

## 3. Bereavement Leave
- Immediate Family: Employees are entitled to up to 5 consecutive paid business days of bereavement leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, domestic partner, child, parent, legal guardian, or sibling).
- Extended Family: Up to 3 consecutive paid business days for grandparents, grandchildren, aunts, uncles, nieces, nephews, or in-laws.
- Additional unpaid leave may be requested through People Partners if international travel is required.

## 4. Short-Term Medical Leave & Leave of Absence (LOA)
- Eligible employees experiencing a serious health condition, non-work-related injury, or surgical recovery may request Short-Term Medical Leave for up to 12 weeks.
- Procedure:
  1. File the formal Leave of Absence in WorkWeek specifying the anticipated start date and duration.
  2. The system triggers an IT ticket in ServiceImmediately to establish temporary email routing and mailbox delegation to the designated manager.
  3. Submit a certified medical certificate to People Operations within 15 calendar days of leave commencement.
- Statutory protections guarantee job restoration to an equivalent position upon medical clearance.
