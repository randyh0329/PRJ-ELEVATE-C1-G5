# Remote & Hybrid Work Guidelines (2026 Edition)
Document ID: POL-HR-REMOTE-2026
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 2.1
Owner: Workplace Solutions & People Operations

## 1. Eligibility for Remote Work
- Employees with approved remote or hybrid contracts documented in WorkWeek are eligible for remote work allowances and equipment procurement.
- Work location must be kept current in WorkWeek employee profile records at all times.

## 2. Home Office Hardware & Monitor Allowance
- Remote-eligible employees may request standard home office hardware through the corporate IT catalog.
- Standard hardware bundle includes:
  - One 27-inch 4K ergonomic display monitor.
  - Standard ergonomic keyboard and wireless mouse.
  - USB-C multiport docking station.
- Procedure for Ordering:
  1. Confirm remote employee status in WorkWeek profile.
  2. Generate a hardware procurement request in ServiceImmediately specifying current shipping address.
  3. IT Operations dispatches approved peripherals with tracking provided via ServiceImmediately.

## 3. Workplace Safety & Ergonomics
- Employees must maintain a designated work area complying with occupational safety and health ergonomic standards.
