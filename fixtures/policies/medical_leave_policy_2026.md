# Short-Term Medical Leave and Disability Guidelines (2026 Edition)
Document ID: POL-HR-MEDLEAVE-2026
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 1.8
Owner: Global People Operations & Health Services

## 1. Scope and Eligibility
- All full-time employees who have completed at least 30 days of active service.
- Covers temporary incapacitation due to illness, surgical procedures, non-occupational injuries, or medical rehabilitation.

## 2. Benefit and Compensation
- Provides up to 12 weeks of leave:
  - First 6 weeks: 100% base salary continuation.
  - Weeks 7 through 12: 80% base salary continuation.
- Medical leave filings are HUMAN_CONSEQUENTIAL enterprise transactions: once accepted by HR, they cannot be retracted automatically by automated systems.

## 3. Initiation Workflow
1. Employee initiates a Leave of Absence in WorkWeek, selecting "Medical Leave" and specifying start and estimated return dates.
2. Concurrent IT provisioning: ServiceImmediately ticket opened to route incoming email communications to the employee's manager to ensure business continuity.
3. Employee uploads certified physician clearance to the secure health portal within 15 calendar days.
