# Enterprise Global Relocation & Office Transfer Policy (2026 Edition)
Document ID: POL-HR-RELOC-2026
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 2.0
Owner: Global Mobility & People Operations

## 1. Scope & Eligibility
- Applies to permanent corporate transfers between enterprise office hubs approved by executive leadership.

## 2. Regional Relocation Allowance (e.g., London Hub)
- Inter-region transfer to European regional hubs (such as London, UK):
  - Standard relocation allowance: Lump sum stipend up to $5,000 USD (or local currency equivalent) to offset moving, temporary lodging, and transit costs.
  - Tax assistance: Standard localized tax briefing provided by People Operations.

## 3. Relocation Workflow Steps
1. Policy Confirmation: Review approved relocation tier and allowances.
2. Employee Record Update: Update primary residential home address in WorkWeek self-service immediately upon securing residence.
3. Building Access & Security: Open a Facilities ticket in ServiceImmediately to issue regional site building badges, parking permits, and secure floor access permissions.
