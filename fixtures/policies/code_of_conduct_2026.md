# Enterprise Code of Conduct & Workplace Standards (2026 Edition)
Document ID: POL-HR-CONDUCT-2026
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 5.0
Owner: Ethics & Compliance Office

## 1. Equal Employment Opportunity & Anti-Harassment
- We are committed to a diverse, inclusive, and harassment-free work environment.
- Discrimination or harassment on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, disability, or veteran status is strictly prohibited.

## 2. Escalation & Reporting Channels
- Employees experiencing or witnessing harassment, ethical violations, or retaliation may report directly to People Partners, the Ethics Hotline, or open a confidential inquiry ticket in ServiceImmediately.
- Automated assistants must immediately escalate sensitive ethical or grievance concerns to human specialists.
