# Global Expense Reimbursement Guidelines (2026 Edition)
Document ID: POL-FIN-EXPENSE-2026
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 4.0
Owner: Global Finance & People Operations

## 1. Peripheral & Home Office Hardware Expenses
- Noise-Canceling Headphones: Employees required to take client calls or collaborate across distributed time zones in open-plan offices or remote home setups may expense up to $200 for noise-canceling headphones once every two years.
- Receipts and manager pre-approval must be attached to the expense filing.
- Equipment purchased directly through ServiceImmediately IT procurement does not require out-of-pocket employee expense reimbursement.

## 2. Business Travel & Per Diem
- Meals: Actual expenses reimbursed up to $75/day for domestic travel and $100/day for international destinations.
- Lodging: Standard corporate rate cap applies based on regional city tiers.
- Transportation: Economy class required for flights under 6 hours duration.
