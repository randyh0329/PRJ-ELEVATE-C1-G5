# Eligibility

* **Eligible:** full-time, part-time and fixed-term Singapore-based employees,
  apprentices and interns.[^hb-20]
* **Not eligible:** temps, vendors and contractors, who must contact their direct
  employer.[^hb-20]

# Accrual

The full year's entitlement is earned on 1 January, not accrued monthly.[^hb-20] The
number of days depends on continuous service:[^hb-20]

| Continuous service | Days per year |
|--------------------|---------------|
| 1 to 6 years       | 20 |
| 7 to 10 years      | 21 |
| 11 years and above | 22 |

* **First year:** prorated on start date.[^hb-20]
* **Part-time and fixed-term:** prorated on working schedule or FTE percentage - 50%
  FTE accrues 50% of the rate.[^hb-1-2]

The calculation is available as an attested computation:
[vacation entitlement](/computations/vacation-entitlement.md).

# Increments and shift workers

* Vacation may be taken in half-days or full days.[^hb-20]
* A vacation day is defined as **8 hours**. A shift longer than 8 hours consumes
  vacation at actual shift hours: a **12-hour shift requires 1.5 vacation
  days**.[^hb-20]

# Booking

* Discuss and obtain manager approval **at least 15 days in advance**.[^hb-20]
* Changes and cancellations must also be made **at least 15 days before** the leave
  starts.[^hb-20]
* Once approved, record the leave in **WorkWeek or gTime**.[^hb-20]
* Requests must be chronologically valid - the start date cannot fall after the end
  date - and the system automatically rejects any request exceeding the current
  accrued balance.[^hb-1-2]

# Carryover and payout

* Unused days carry over for **exactly one additional year** and must be used by
  31 December of that following year or they are forfeited.[^hb-20]
* Altostrat does **not** cash out unused vacation, so that employees take real
  breaks. The only exceptions are country-to-country transfer and leaving the
  company.[^hb-20]
* On separation, the current year's unused balance **is** paid out, prorated on time
  worked in the year, plus any eligible carryover balance.[^hb-29-2]
* A **negative balance** - more vacation taken than earned - is deducted from the
  final paycheck.[^hb-20]

# Floating holidays

* A public holiday falling on a Saturday or Sunday earns a floating
  holiday.[^hb-20]
* Floating holidays are used like vacation and must be taken before the calendar year
  ends.[^hb-20]
* They do **not** carry forward and are **not** cashed out on transfer or
  termination.[^hb-20]
* Employees are recommended to use floating holidays before their standard vacation
  balance.[^hb-20]

# During leave

Salary continues as normal, with no impact on the company bonus, which is still
calculated from the GRAD rating. Stock continues to vest. Health, life, long-term
disability and travel insurance, retirement contributions, gym reimbursement and home
internet reimbursement all continue. Vacation is **not** extended by public holidays
falling inside it.[^hb-20]

# Gaps in the source

* The **proration formula** for a first-year employee is not specified - the handbook
  says only "based on your start date". The attested computation encodes a calendar-day
  proportion and flags the assumption.
* The **rounding rule** for prorated part-day results is not specified.
* Which tier a **year-zero** employee prorates against is not stated; the computation
  assumes the 20-day tier.
