# Eligibility

* **Eligible:** full-time, part-time and fixed-term Singapore-based employees,
  apprentices and interns.[^hb-24]
* **Not eligible:** temps, vendors and contractors.[^hb-24]
* The benefit is **inclusive of** all local statutory entitlements.[^hb-24]

# Allowance

For parents of children **under 12**:[^hb-24]

| Children's ages | Paid days per year |
|-----------------|--------------------|
| All children under 7 | 6 |
| Youngest child 7 to 12 | 2 |
| Children in both age bands | 6 |

Leave is counted in **full work days** against the regular schedule - there are no
half-days.[^hb-24]

# Requesting

Discuss and agree dates with your manager, then enter the leave in **WorkWeek**. The
manager is notified that the booking exists.[^hb-24]

# During leave

Salary continues as if working normal hours and stock continues to vest. Vacation
continues to accrue. Childcare leave need not be applied for on a public holiday unless
that holiday is a working day for you. Health, life, long-term disability and travel
insurance, retirement contributions, gym reimbursement and internet reimbursement
continue. Corporate system access should be minimal and may be suspended if you work
during leave. Intellectual property created during leave belongs to the company, subject
to the exceptions in your signed agreements. Notify your manager of your return-to-work
date.[^hb-24]

# Carryover, separation and transfer

* Unused childcare leave does **not** carry over and is **not** paid out on
  leaving.[^hb-1-3]
* Leave stops on the End Date.[^hb-24]
* On transfer to another country, check the benefits portal for the new
  entitlement.[^hb-24]
