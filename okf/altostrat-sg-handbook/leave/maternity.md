> **Unsettled in the source.** The SPL interaction with
> [baby bonding leave](/leave/baby-bonding.md) is stated three different ways, and intern
> entitlement turns on a one-word difference worth 16 weeks. This concept records each
> reading rather than choosing; see **Conflict** below.

# Eligibility

* **Eligible:** full-time, part-time and fixed-term Singapore-based employees and
  interns.[^hb-27]
* **Not eligible:** temps, vendors and contractors.[^hb-27]
* The benefit is **inclusive of** all local statutory entitlements for birthing
  mothers.[^hb-27]

# Duration

**24 weeks of paid maternity leave**, effective 1 April 2026.[^hb-27]

# Shared Parental Leave

Under Phase 2 of Singapore's MSF scheme, parents of Singaporean children born on or
after **1 April 2026** receive **10 weeks of Shared Parental Leave**, split **5 weeks
each** by default.[^hb-2-1]

| Scenario | Effect on maternity leave |
|----------|---------------------------|
| Spouse donates 9 or 10 weeks of SPL to you | Extends to **25 or 26 weeks**[^hb-27] |
| You donate 9 or 10 weeks of SPL to your spouse | Stays at **24 weeks** - the company benefit already exceeds statutory limits[^hb-27] |
| Both parents are employees, mother receives spouse's SPL | Extends to **25 or 26 weeks**[^hb-27] |

**Verification.** Regardless of the arrangement, submit proof of the official SPL
allocation from **LifeSG within 4 weeks** of the birth or adoption date. Altostrat may
**claw back** additional leave granted if the official arrangement differs from the
documentation submitted.[^hb-27]

# Interns and part-time employees

* **Part-time:** entitled to maternity benefits, with salary prorated on the contracted
  schedule.[^hb-27]
* **Interns:** those with at least **3 months' continuous service** are entitled to
  **16 weeks of statutory maternity leave**, which must be taken
  **consecutively**.[^hb-27] An intern whose spouse donates SPL can extend to a total of
  **26 weeks**.[^hb-2-1] Note that Section 27.3 phrases this as extending "**by** up to
  26 weeks", which would give 42 weeks; that reading is inconsistent with the 16-week
  base plus a 10-week SPL maximum, and with Section 2.1's "up to 26 weeks".

# Planning and requesting

* Earliest start: **28 days before the expected due date**.[^hb-27]
* The first **8 weeks (56 days) must be taken consecutively**.[^hb-27]
* The remaining **16 weeks (80 working days)** may be taken flexibly, in daily
  increments, across the **12 months** following the birth.[^hb-27]
* Give your manager at least **4 weeks' notice** of leave dates.[^hb-27]
* Record in WorkWeek under **two separate codes**:[^hb-27]
  * `Singapore Leaves > SG - Maternity Leave (First 8 weeks)`
  * `Singapore Leaves > SG - Maternity Leave (80 working days)`

# During leave

Salary continues as if working normal hours, and you remain eligible for the company or
sales bonus calculated on on-target multipliers. Stock continues to vest and vacation
continues to accrue. Leave is **not** extended by public holidays falling inside it.
Health, life, disability and travel insurance, retirement contributions, gym
reimbursement and internet reimbursement continue.[^hb-27]

# Returning

Up to **2 weeks of [ramp-back time](/leave/ramp-back.md)** is available, plus access to
onsite lactation rooms. Corporate system access should be minimal during leave and may
be suspended if you work regularly. Intellectual property created during leave belongs
to the company. Notify your manager of your return-to-work date. On leaving, leave stops
on the End Date with **no payout** of unused maternity leave.[^hb-27]

# Conflict: does a father's SPL donation reduce his baby bonding leave?

The handbook answers this three ways:

| Source | Answer |
|--------|--------|
| Section 27.2[^hb-27] | Employee fathers allocating 9 or 10 weeks of SPL to their partner **must reduce** baby bonding leave to 16 or 17 weeks |
| Section 2.2[^hb-2-1] | Same - **reduced** to 16 or 17 weeks |
| Section 26.3[^hb-26-3] | Both parents employees: the father's baby bonding leave **remains 18 weeks and is not reduced** |

Section 26.3 directly contradicts the other two. Do not answer this question from the
corpus; route it to People Ops. Logged in the
[source defect register](/references/source-defects.md).
