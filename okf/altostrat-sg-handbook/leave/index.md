# Singapore leave

* [Sick and hospitalisation leave](sick-and-hospitalisation.md) - 14 days outpatient plus 46 days hospitalisation, MC rules and excessive-usage monitoring.
* [Vacation leave](vacation.md) - service-tiered annual accrual, 15-day booking notice, one-year carryover and floating holidays.
* [Childcare leave](childcare.md) - 2 to 6 days a year for parents of children under 12.
* [Time off in lieu](time-off-in-lieu.md) - compensation for required public-holiday or weekend work, at manager discretion.
* [Maternity leave](maternity.md) - 24 weeks, Shared Parental Leave extension to 26 weeks, and the two WorkWeek leave codes.

# Global leave

* [Baby bonding leave](baby-bonding.md) - 18 weeks for non-birthing parents through birth, adoption, surrogacy or fostering.
* [Ramp-back time](ramp-back.md) - 2 weeks at full pay on a 50% schedule after a long parental leave.
* [Bereavement leave](bereavement.md) - 4 weeks per event, including pregnancy loss.
* [Carer's leave](carers.md) - 8 weeks per loved one per lifetime for serious or terminal illness.
* [Unpaid time off and personal leave](unpaid-and-personal.md) - 30 days unpaid, extending to a 92-day personal leave.
* [Global leaves overview](global-leaves-overview.md) - the handbook's own cross-country summary of every leave type.
