`status: draft` because the source gives two different upper bounds for personal leave
and disagrees on whether the qualifying criteria are requirements or discretionary
factors. See **Conflicts** below.

# Eligibility

* **Unpaid time off and personal leave:** full-time, part-time and fixed-term
  employees.[^hb-18]
* **Interns and apprentices:** unpaid time off **only** - they may **not** take personal
  leave.[^hb-18]

# Unpaid time off - up to 30 days

* With **manager approval**, up to **30 calendar days**.[^hb-18]
* Taken continuously or in daily increments.[^hb-18]
* Prorated for part-time employees on their work schedule.[^hb-18]
* After verbal or written manager approval, enter it in **WorkWeek**
  yourself.[^hb-18]
* Extending beyond 30 continuous calendar days requires a **new request**, and if
  approved the **entire leave is reclassified as personal leave**.[^hb-18]

# Personal leave - up to 92 days

* Requires **manager and director approval**.[^hb-18]
* Up to **92 calendar days continuous**, **inclusive of** any unpaid time off already
  taken.[^hb-18]
* Must be taken in **one continuous block**. Vacation can be added only before or after
  it.[^hb-18]
* **Notice:** at least **30 days in advance** to your manager.[^hb-18]
* **Booking:** you cannot enter this yourself. Contact support with the manager and
  director approvals, and People Ops enters the leave in WorkWeek on your
  behalf.[^hb-18]
* Recommended to exhaust accrued vacation, medical leave and carer's leave
  first.[^hb-18]

**Approval criteria.** Applications are evaluated on past performance and current team
needs, case by case at the business's discretion. Managers and directors **may
consider**: at least 2 years' tenure; a "Significant Impact" or higher rating in the last
cycle; fewer than 10 vacation days remaining; and the team's ability to cover the
work.[^hb-18]

# Unpaid compassionate leave

For serious personal reasons - caring for a seriously or terminally ill loved one,
grieving a loss, or a major family transition such as divorce or relationship breakdown -
up to **92 calendar days continuous**.[^hb-18]

* Requires manager and/or director approval.[^hb-18]
* **Inclusive of** any unpaid time off already taken.[^hb-18]
* **Cannot be stacked** with unpaid personal leave.[^hb-18]

# Conditions during leave

* **Pay and tax:** unpaid, including for holidays falling inside the leave. You may
  remain liable for income tax on continuing benefits and insurances, which Altostrat
  deducts from the **first paycheck after your return**.[^hb-18]
* **Access:** keep office visits minimal outside pre-arranged wellness services.
  Corporate system access should be minimal and may be **suspended** if you access
  systems regularly or attempt to work.[^hb-18]
* **Intellectual property:** you remain subject to your employment contract and the
  Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement; IP created during leave
  belongs to the company unless the agreement's exceptions apply.[^hb-18]
* **Visa holders** may face limits on how much unpaid leave they can take and **must
  contact the immigration team** to understand the effect on immigration
  status.[^hb-18]

# Inappropriate use

* Not a substitute for a flexible work arrangement or a physician-required workplace
  accommodation.[^hb-18]
* **Not for starting a business or working for someone else.** Exceptions are at
  Altostrat's sole discretion, generally reserved for business-critical situations, and
  require approval from the **VP, your manager and the Ethics and Compliance team before**
  the leave or outside employment starts.[^hb-18]

# Conflicts

**1. Upper bound on personal leave.**

| Source | Bound |
|--------|-------|
| Sections 18.3 and 3.3[^hb-18] | Up to **92 calendar days** |
| Section 21.5[^hb-21-5] | "31 calendar days or more, but **less than 90** calendar days" |

92 and "less than 90" cannot both be right. A request for 91 days is approvable under one
and refused under the other.

**2. Are the qualifying criteria binding?**

| Source | Framing |
|--------|---------|
| Section 3.3[^hb-3-3] | "You **typically need** at least 2 years of tenure and ... a 'Significant Impact' or higher rating ... **to qualify**" - a prerequisite |
| Section 18.3[^hb-18] | Managers and directors "**may consider**" those same factors - discretionary |

Section 3.3 also states it is "**highly recommended** that you have fewer than 10 vacation
days remaining **before unpaid leaves are approved**", which reads as a gate on approval
rather than a factor.

Neither conflict is resolved here. Both are logged in the
[source defect register](/references/source-defects.md).
