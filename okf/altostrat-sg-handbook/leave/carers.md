# Purpose and eligibility

Paid leave to care for, or arrange care for, people close to you who are **seriously or
terminally ill** - family members, partners and dependents.[^hb-23]

* **Eligible:** full-time and part-time employees.[^hb-23]
* **Not eligible:** apprentices, interns, temps, vendors and contractors.[^hb-23]

# Allowance

* Up to **8 weeks of paid leave per loved one, per lifetime**.[^hb-23] This is a
  lifetime cap scoped to the individual being cared for, not an annual entitlement.
* Counted in work days against the regular schedule.[^hb-23]
* Requested in **weekly blocks** or **daily schedules** - for example one day a week
  over a period.[^hb-23]
* **Minimum duration: half a work day.**[^hb-23]
* **Proration:** part-time employees are prorated by FTE - 50% FTE gives up to 20 leave
  days.[^hb-23]

# Attestation and privacy

* You may be asked to **acknowledge in writing** that you are needed to provide care or
  comfort to a loved one with a serious or terminal illness. Altostrat may request medical
  provider documentation confirming this.[^hb-23]
* **Privacy guardrail: do not share any medical information when you contact
  support.**[^hb-23] The summary layer repeats this: do not share sensitive medical
  details.[^hb-3-2]

This guardrail is a hard constraint on any system handling these requests - the request
channel must be able to accept a carer's leave application without medical detail
entering it.

# Requesting

Contact your manager to discuss. Afterwards, you or your manager contacts support with
the leave or flexibility needs, **exact dates**, and your **relationship** to the person
being cared for. Once approved, the leave is entered in WorkWeek on your behalf.[^hb-23]

# During leave

Salary continues as if working normal hours and stock continues to vest. Vacation
continues to accrue. Do not schedule a carer's leave day on a company holiday. Medical,
life, travel and disability insurance and retirement contributions continue, and you
remain responsible for benefits deductions, taken from leave pay as normal. Gym benefits,
commuter benefits, company-paid cell phone reimbursement and internet reimbursement
continue.[^hb-23]

Keep office visits and corporate system access minimal; access may be suspended if you
work regularly. You remain subject to your employment contract and the Confidential
Information and Invention Assignment Agreement, and intellectual property created during
leave belongs to the company subject to the agreement's exceptions.[^hb-23]

# Returning and separation

Notify your manager of your return-to-work date. If you are not returning, or are
unsure, discuss it with your manager and contact support as soon as possible. On leaving,
carer's leave stops on the End Date with **no payout** of the unused
balance.[^hb-23]
