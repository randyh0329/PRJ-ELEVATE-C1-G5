# Eligibility

* **Eligible:** full-time, part-time and fixed-term Singapore-based employees and
  interns.[^hb-25]
* **Not eligible:** temps, vendors and contractors.[^hb-25]
* The benefit is **inclusive of** all local statutory entitlements.[^hb-25]

# Earning TOIL

TOIL compensates for working outside contractual hours when **the business requires**
you to work on a public holiday or at the weekend.[^hb-25]

It is granted **at your manager's discretion**. If you believe you have a case - for
example you worked a public holiday because the business needed it - raise it with your
manager.[^hb-25]

# Taking TOIL

* Agree the dates with your manager.[^hb-25]
* **Use TOIL days before logging any additional vacation days.**[^hb-1-4]
* **Do not log TOIL in WorkWeek.** Unlike vacation, TOIL has no system entry; the
  agreement with your manager is the whole mechanism.[^hb-25]

# What the source does not say

The handbook gives TOIL no accrual rate, no expiry, no cap and no separation payout
rule. It also provides no record of a TOIL balance anywhere, since nothing is entered in
WorkWeek. Questions about how much TOIL someone has, or what happens to it on exit,
cannot be answered from this corpus.
