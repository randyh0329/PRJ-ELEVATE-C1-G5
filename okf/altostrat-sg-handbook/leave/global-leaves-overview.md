This is the handbook's own high-level survey of leave across countries. **It is
orientation material, not the governing text.** Exact length and payment vary by country,
and other local statutory leaves may exist depending on location.[^hb-21] For any actual
entitlement question, use the specific policy concept.

# Paid time off

* **[Vacation](/leave/vacation.md):** paid time off to rest and spend time with friends
  and family. Accrued vacation is tracked in **WorkWeek**.[^hb-21]
* **Public holidays:** each work location observes certain company-recognised paid public
  holidays. A religious holiday that is not a public holiday can be covered with vacation,
  or with unpaid time off if vacation is insufficient.[^hb-21]

# Health and care

* **[Sick time](/leave/sick-and-hospitalisation.md):** paid time off to recover from
  illness or injury. **You do not have to tell your manager or team the specific medical
  reason.**[^hb-21]
* **[Carer's leave](/leave/carers.md):** paid leave to care for a seriously ill family
  member or loved one, extendable with other leaves or flexibility
  arrangements.[^hb-21]

# Family-building and transition

* **[Maternity leave](/leave/maternity.md):** approximately **24 weeks at 100%
  pay**.[^hb-21]
* **[Baby bonding leave](/leave/baby-bonding.md):** **18 weeks at 100% pay**, in one-week
  increments within the first year of birth or adoption.[^hb-21]
* **[Ramp-back time](/leave/ramp-back.md):** **2 weeks at 100% pay while working 50%
  time**. Requires at least 10 consecutive weeks of bonding or maternity leave, must be
  taken immediately on return, and the schedule is customisable provided you work at least
  50% of normal hours.[^hb-21]

# Unpaid

* **Unpaid time off:** 30 calendar days or less, used when extra leave is needed beyond
  other entitlements - extending baby bonding leave, or an exhausted vacation
  balance.[^hb-21]
* **Personal leave:** "31 calendar days or more, but less than 90 calendar days", for
  extending baby bonding leave, study or travel.[^hb-21]

# Known defects in this section

* The **90-day** figure above contradicts the **92-day** limit stated twice in Sections
  18.3 and 3.3. See [unpaid and personal leave](/leave/unpaid-and-personal.md).
* Section 21.3 refers to extending "your **career's** leave" - a typo for **carer's**
  leave.[^hb-21]

Both are logged in the [source defect register](/references/source-defects.md).
