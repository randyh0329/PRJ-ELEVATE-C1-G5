# Eligibility

* **Eligible:** Singapore-based employees and interns who are sick, or whom a doctor
  certifies unfit for work.[^hb-19]
* **Not eligible:** temps, vendors and contractors.[^hb-19]
* The entitlement is **inclusive of** all local statutory leave. Part-time and
  fixed-term employees are prorated on working hours.[^hb-19]

# Outpatient sick leave

Up to **14 days per calendar year** at **100% of base salary**, counted in work days
against the regular schedule.[^hb-19]

# Hospitalisation leave

An additional **46 work days**.[^hb-19] This is **not** an automatic extension of
outpatient sick leave - it is discretionary, subject to business approval, and granted
case by case.[^hb-19] Contact support and meet one of four criteria:[^hb-19]

1. Warded as an inpatient, or for day surgery.
2. Under quarantine under any written law, in hospital or not.
3. Ill enough to require hospitalisation during the leave period, certified by a
   medical practitioner.
4. Needing rest or further treatment after discharge, certified by a medical
   practitioner.

**Certification.** The sick certificate must state the hospitalisation leave period and
be issued by a registered medical practitioner able to admit patients to hospital. For
criteria (3) and (4) the practitioner must be employed by a hospital approved by the
Minister for Manpower; otherwise Altostrat may request more information before
approving.[^hb-19]

# Notification and medical certificates

* Tell your manager **at least one hour before your normal start time**. If the manager
  is unavailable, contact support.[^hb-19]
* Also notify your manager of your expected return-to-work date.[^hb-19]
* Sick for **more than two work days** - submit the sick certificate from a registered
  medical practitioner through **WorkWeek** within **48 hours**.[^hb-19]
* Altostrat may decline the leave, or classify it unpaid, if the certificate is not
  submitted.[^hb-1-1]
* Enter sick time in WorkWeek even if the team keeps a separate leave calendar, or pay
  and support cannot be processed correctly.[^hb-19]
* Absence with no reason given counts as **absent without approval**, may be unpaid,
  and can lead to disciplinary action.[^hb-19]

# Medical appointments

Appointments may be scheduled during work hours for a reasonable time. Tell your
manager as early as possible and agree the duration. Depending on length you may be
asked to log it as sick leave in WorkWeek and provide a certificate confirming the
appointment.[^hb-19]

# During leave

Salary continues as if working normal hours and stock continues to vest. Vacation
continues to accrue. Sick leave need not be applied for on a public holiday unless that
holiday is a working day for you. Medical, life, travel and disability insurance,
retirement contributions, gym reimbursement and internet reimbursement continue.
Corporate system access should be minimal; Altostrat may suspend access if you work
regularly during leave. Intellectual property created during leave belongs to the
company, subject to the exceptions in your signed agreements.[^hb-19]

# Excessive usage

Altostrat monitors sick time. Repeated, regular or high absence will be discussed with
you. You may be asked to seek medical advice, attend an assessment, or submit a
certificate from the first day of every new absence period. Sick time must not be used
to supplement vacation or public holidays; misuse may lead to investigation and
discipline up to dismissal.[^hb-19]

# Leaving or transferring

Leave stops on the End Date. **Unused sick and hospitalisation leave is never paid
out**, and balances do **not** transfer to another location.[^hb-19]

# Conflict: system of record

Section 19.4 and Section 1.1 both name **WorkWeek** as the system for MC submission.
The onboarding section instead instructs new joiners to submit the MC in
**Workday**.[^hb-30-onboarding] Only one can be the system of record. This bundle does
not resolve it - see the
[source defect register](/references/source-defects.md).
