# Purpose and eligibility

Paid leave to spend time with a new child arriving through **childbirth, adoption,
surrogacy or fostering**. It does **not** apply to birth-giving parents, who take
[maternity leave](/leave/maternity.md) instead.[^hb-26]

* **Eligible:** full-time, part-time and fixed-term global employees who are an
  expectant parent through adoption, surrogacy or fostering, or the partner of an
  expectant parent.[^hb-26]
* **Not eligible:** apprentices, interns, temps, vendors and contractors.[^hb-26]

# Allowance and timelines

* **18 weeks (90 work days)** of paid leave.[^hb-26] The source says "per year", which
  sits oddly with the one-period-per-event rules below - see **Gaps** .
* **Inclusive of** all local statutory entitlements, including statutory paternity
  leave.[^hb-26]
* **Multiples:** welcoming several children at once - multiple birth, simultaneous
  adoptions - still yields only **one 18-week period**.[^hb-26]
* **Window:** leave starts on or after the birth, adoption or foster placement date and
  must be **fully used within 12 months** of it. Any remainder is
  forfeited.[^hb-26]
* **Increments:** counted in work days; taken all at once or in **weekly
  blocks**.[^hb-26]

# Requesting

* Give your manager at least **4 weeks' notice** where possible.[^hb-26]
* Enter the leave in **WorkWeek or gTime**.[^hb-26]
* Documentation is not required at request time, but Altostrat may later ask for a
  birth or adoption certificate.[^hb-26]
* For a planned medical leave running beyond a standard work week, open an
  administrative ticket - **Category `HRSD`, Priority `3 - Moderate`** - to request
  temporary email delegation to your direct manager.[^hb-2-2]

# During leave

Salary continues as if working normal hours and stock continues to vest. Vacation
continues to accrue; in select locations leave is not extended by public holidays
falling inside it, and extra days are added to the balance instead. Enrolled benefits,
gym benefits, company-paid cell phone reimbursement and internet reimbursement
continue.[^hb-26]

**Baby bonding benefit.** A monetary benefit is payable, to spend on meals, house
cleaning, diapers, laundry or gardening services.[^hb-26]

# Returning

Corporate system access should be minimal and may be suspended if you work regularly
during leave. Notify your manager of your return-to-work date. Taking **at least 10
consecutive weeks** of baby bonding leave makes you eligible for
[ramp-back time](/leave/ramp-back.md).[^hb-26]

# Conflict: Singapore Shared Parental Leave

Effective 1 April 2026 the SPL scheme provides 10 weeks of parental leave, and the
18-week baby bonding entitlement is inclusive of it.[^hb-26] What happens when an
employee father donates SPL to his partner is stated three incompatible ways:

| Source | Answer |
|--------|--------|
| Section 26.3[^hb-26] | Both parents employees, birthing parent donates SPL to the father: the father's BBL **remains 18 weeks**. Birthing parent uses SPL to extend maternity leave: father's BBL **remains 18 weeks and is not reduced** |
| Section 27.2[^hb-27-2] | Employee fathers allocating 9 or 10 weeks of SPL to their partner **must reduce** BBL to **16 or 17 weeks**, subject to periodic audits |
| Section 2.2[^hb-2-2] | Same reduction to **16 or 17 weeks**, subject to periodic audits |

Do not answer this from the corpus. Route to People Ops. Logged in the
[source defect register](/references/source-defects.md).

The uncontested SPL scenarios are:[^hb-26]

* **Employee father, non-employee mother:** father's entitlement stays 18 weeks
  regardless of how SPL is shared.
* **Non-employee father, employee mother:** if the partner donates 9 or 10 weeks, the
  mother's maternity leave may extend to 25 or 26 weeks.

**Verification.** Submit the SPL arrangement via **LifeSG** and provide a copy to
Altostrat.[^hb-26]

# Gaps in the source

* "18 weeks **per year**" conflicts with "only one 18-week period" for multiples and
  with the 12-month single-window rule. Whether a second child in the same calendar year
  triggers a fresh 18 weeks is not answerable from the source.
* Section 2.2 says baby bonding leave is for "parents who do not take maternity leave",
  while Section 26.1 excludes "birth-giving parents". These are not the same set - an
  adoptive parent who declines maternity leave is treated differently by the two
  phrasings.
