# Purpose

Paid leave to grieve and support loved ones when someone close to you dies. This
**includes pregnancy loss through miscarriage or stillbirth**.[^hb-22]

**Pet loss** is not covered. Arrange vacation, unpaid time off, or a temporary flexible
schedule with your manager instead.[^hb-22]

# Allowance

* Up to **4 weeks of paid leave per event**.[^hb-22]
* Counted in work days against the regular schedule - a 40-hour, 5-day week gives
  **20 work days**.[^hb-22]
* **Inclusive of** all local statutory leave requirements.[^hb-22]
* Must be taken **within 12 months of the death**. Exceptions are possible where
  religious, spiritual or cultural practice puts funeral rites at a later
  time.[^hb-22]

# Requesting

Contact your manager, or support, to discuss the situation and share your leave or
flexibility needs, including any travel time required.[^hb-22]

# During leave

Salary and benefits continue as if working normal hours; you remain responsible for
benefits deductions. Keep office visits and corporate system access minimal - Altostrat
may suspend access if you work regularly during leave. You remain an employee, so
intellectual property created during leave belongs to the company, subject to the
exceptions in your signed agreements.[^hb-22]

# Returning to work

* Notify your manager of your return-to-work date. If you do not plan to return, give
  notice of termination as your contract requires.[^hb-22]
* **Adjusted expectations.** Loss affects capacity well beyond the leave itself.
  Employees and managers are directed to **document adjusted expectations scaled to the
  individual's changed capacity** after the event.[^hb-22]

# Support

* Confidential counselling for employees and dependents through the Employee Assistance
  Programme provider, **Lyra**.[^hb-22]
* Peer support spaces for bereaved co-workers, for parents who have lost a child, and
  for general mental health awareness.[^hb-22]

# Conflict: are interns and apprentices eligible?

| Source | Answer |
|--------|--------|
| Section 22.1[^hb-22] | Full-time and part-time employees eligible. **Apprentices and interns are not eligible** under this policy, though they may have a statutory entitlement by location. Temps, vendors and contractors not eligible |
| Section 3.1[^hb-3-1] | "Employees can take up to 4 weeks" - states **no exclusions at all** |

The detail layer governs under the bundle's precedence convention, so interns and
apprentices should be treated as excluded. Because the summary layer is silent rather
than contradictory, this is the weaker class of conflict - but an intern asking directly
should be routed to People Ops rather than refused on the strength of this corpus.
Logged in the [source defect register](/references/source-defects.md).
