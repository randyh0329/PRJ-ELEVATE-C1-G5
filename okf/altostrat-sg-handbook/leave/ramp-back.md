# Purpose and eligibility

Up to **2 weeks on a reduced schedule at full salary**, easing the return from
maternity, adoption, fostering or baby bonding leave.[^hb-28]

* **Eligible:** full-time, part-time and fixed-term employees who took **at least 10
  consecutive weeks** of maternity, adoption, parental or baby bonding leave.[^hb-28]
* **Not eligible:** interns, temps, vendors and contractors.[^hb-28] Note the summary
  layer states the 10-week rule without mentioning the intern exclusion;[^hb-2-3] the
  detail layer governs.
* **One period only**, even for multiple children at once or intermittent leave.[^hb-28]

# Schedule

* Must be taken **immediately, in the first 2 weeks of your return to work**. If you
  extended your parental leave with vacation, parental leave or unpaid time off, ramp-back
  still attaches to the first 2 weeks of the eventual return.[^hb-28]
* Work a **minimum of 50% of normal weekly hours** in each of the two weeks.[^hb-28]
* **Holidays:** a single holiday in the period reduces the usual work week - for example
  40 hours to 32 - and you work half of the reduced figure, 16 hours. The holiday counts
  as one of your ramp-back days.[^hb-28]

# Pay and benefits

* **100% of normal weekly salary.**[^hb-28]
* If you and your manager agree to continue **part-time permanently** after the leave,
  you are paid 100% of the **new part-time salary** for the two weeks.[^hb-28]
* Bonus, benefits and vacation accrual continue.[^hb-28]

# System entry

Ramp-back is recorded as the hours **not** worked, not the hours worked.[^hb-28]

* **Salaried:** in WorkWeek, select the days not worked in whole or part, choose type
  **`Ramp Back Time`**, and enter reason **`Baby Bonding Leave`**.[^hb-28]
* **Hourly:** on the gTime timecard, enter the time and choose **`Ramp Back
  Time`**.[^hb-28]

Discuss the schedule with your manager **before** you return.[^hb-28]

# After ramp-back

You may use the two weeks of ramp-back and then move to a longer-term gradual
return-to-work or part-time schedule. That ongoing arrangement needs separate manager
approval - see [flexible work requests](/workplace/flexible-work-requests.md).[^hb-28]

# Note on the reason code

The prescribed WorkWeek reason is always `Baby Bonding Leave`, even for someone
returning from maternity leave. The source states this without qualification; it appears
to be a system constraint rather than a statement about which leave was taken.[^hb-28]
