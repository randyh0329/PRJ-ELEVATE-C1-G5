# Computations

Attested computations under OKF §10. Each carries a `runtime`, declared `parameters`, an
executable `computation`, an `executor` procedure and an `attester` that can verify a
receipt.

* [Annual vacation entitlement (Singapore)](vacation-entitlement.md) - days credited on 1 January from years of service and FTE percentage, with first-year proration. **Draft:** four of its rules are producer assumptions with no basis in the handbook.

# Why there is only one

A computation is only worth attesting where the handbook states a **complete, deterministic
rule**. Most of its numeric provisions do not qualify:

* **Sick and hospitalisation leave** - fixed day counts, no calculation.
* **Baby bonding and maternity** - the SPL interaction is [internally contradictory](/leave/baby-bonding.md); the arithmetic cannot be pinned down.
* **Gift approval tiers** - the [boundaries overlap](/ethics/commercial-gifts.md), so US$250 and US$500 each fall into two tiers. A computation would have to invent a tie-break.
* **Expense caps and aged-claim thresholds** - simple comparisons, and the regional route caps are never stated.
* **CPF** - the handbook deliberately defers to the CPF Board's prevailing rates. No figures to compute with.

Vacation is the one case with a real tiered function - and even it required four assumptions
to make executable. That ratio is itself a finding about the source.
