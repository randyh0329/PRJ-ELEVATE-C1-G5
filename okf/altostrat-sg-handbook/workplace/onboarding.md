# Phase 1: First week essentials - setup and security

**Equipment and hardware**[^hb-30-onboarding]

* Order monitors, security keys, software and other hardware **exclusively through the
  internal ITSM case**.
* IT support is available for setup and remote-work tooling.

**Physical and network security**[^hb-30-onboarding]

* **Wear your badge visibly at all times** on Altostrat premises. Do not tamper with or
  disable security and safety devices. **Report anyone in a secure space without a badge to
  Security immediately.**
* You are bound by the **Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement**
  signed on hiring. Any IP you create during employment belongs to Altostrat.
* Working remotely: no confidential work in public settings; use privacy screens; wear
  headphones in virtual meetings; keep sensitive documents secured.

Governing: [confidentiality and assets](/ethics/confidentiality-and-assets.md),
[remote and hybrid work](/workplace/remote-and-hybrid-work.md).

# Phase 2: Core ethics and mandatory training

* **Mandatory training.** Complete all assigned compliance and role-specific training **in a
  timely fashion**.[^hb-30-onboarding]
* **Anti-bribery.** *Don't bribe.* Never offer, give or receive anything of value to or from
  a government official **or commercial partner** to obtain an improper advantage. Any gift,
  meal or item of value to a government official requires **written RCI pre-approval**, via
  the channel given here as the placeholder `email`.[^hb-30-onboarding]
* **Commercial gifts.** Must be moderate; **anything over US$100 requires manager
  pre-approval**; strict frequency limits apply.[^hb-30-onboarding]
* **Conflicts of interest.** Avoid conflicts and their appearance. Outside employment,
  significant investment in a competitor, or a personal relationship with a vendor you manage
  must be disclosed to your manager and reviewed by **EBI**, at the placeholder `company
  intranet`.[^hb-30-onboarding]

Governing: [anti-bribery](/ethics/anti-bribery.md),
[commercial gifts](/ethics/commercial-gifts.md),
[conflicts of interest](/ethics/conflicts-of-interest.md).

# Phase 3: Navigating the workplace and culture

* **Community guidelines.** Focus on the work; raging debates over politics, the news or
  non-work topics are prohibited. **Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, bullying,
  trolling or doxxing.** Report concerns to your manager, HR, the **Respect@** team, or
  anonymously via the **Compliance Helpline** at the placeholder
  `email`.[^hb-30-onboarding]
* **Personal relationships.** Prohibited where one person supervises or exercises authority
  over the other, **including dotted lines and project leads**. Report a potentially
  conflicting relationship to HR immediately. **VP level and above must disclose all such
  relationships regardless of reporting lines.**[^hb-30-onboarding]
* **Alcohol, smoking and drugs.** Illegal drugs - **including marijuana, regardless of local
  law** - are prohibited at work and work events. Alcohol is not banned at company events but
  the Code of Conduct applies at all times; excessive consumption is not permitted. Smoking
  and vaping are prohibited **inside company buildings and on company
  shuttles**.[^hb-30-onboarding]

Governing: [community guidelines](/conduct/community-guidelines.md),
[harassment and discrimination](/conduct/harassment-and-discrimination.md),
[personal relationships](/conduct/personal-relationships.md),
[alcohol, smoking and drugs](/conduct/alcohol-smoking-drugs.md).

# Phase 4: Time off and leaves

* **Vacation.** First year is **prorated based on your start date**. Obtain manager approval
  **at least 15 days in advance** and log approved time off in **Workday**.[^hb-30-onboarding]
* **Floating holidays.** A public holiday falling on a weekend yields a floating holiday, to
  be used **before the end of the calendar year**.[^hb-30-onboarding]
* **Sick time.** Notify your manager **at least one hour before your normal start time**.
  Sick **more than two consecutive work days** ⇒ submit an **MC from a registered
  practitioner in Workday within 48 hours**.[^hb-30-onboarding]
* **Other leaves.** Maternity, Baby Bonding, Childcare, Carer's and Bereavement leave are
  described in the handbook or at the placeholder `company
  intranet`.[^hb-30-onboarding]

Governing: [vacation](/leave/vacation.md),
[sick and hospitalisation](/leave/sick-and-hospitalisation.md), and the
[leave index](/leave/index.md).

# Phase 5: Travel and expenses

* Flights and hotels **at least 3 weeks in advance**, through the **TRIPS
  portal**.[^hb-30-onboarding]
* **Corporate card** required for **>US$10,000 per quarter** or single transactions **over
  US$5,000**.[^hb-30-onboarding]
* Expenses through **Concur within 30 days** of travel; individual and group meals capped at
  **US$120 per employee per day**.[^hb-30-onboarding]

Governing: [travel and expense](/workplace/travel-and-expense.md).

# Next steps for your first week

1. Log into `ITSM and open a case` to order necessary equipment.
2. Complete mandatory compliance and security training modules.
3. Review benefits and leave entitlements at `company intranet`.
4. Set up a brief sync with your manager on schedule, remote-work arrangements and initial
   project goals.[^hb-30-onboarding]

Items 1 and 3 are reproduced verbatim; the backticked strings are **unresolved placeholders
in the source**, not real destinations.
