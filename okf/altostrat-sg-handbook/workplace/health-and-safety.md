# Safety commitment

Altostrat is committed to maintaining a **safe, healthy and violence-free** work environment
in compliance with the **Singapore Workplace Safety and Health Act (WSHA)**.[^hb-34]

# Emergency procedures

Employees must familiarise themselves with the **evacuation routes, emergency exits and
assembly points** for their specific office location. **Do not tamper with safety equipment
or fire doors.**[^hb-34]

The parallel rule in [confidentiality and assets](/ethics/confidentiality-and-assets.md) -
never tamper with or disable security and safety devices - is a conduct offence as well as a
safety one.

# Reporting accidents and WICA

* **Incident reporting.** Any **workplace accident, injury or potential safety hazard** must
  be reported **immediately** to **your manager and the Workplace Facilities team** via the
  Company Website.[^hb-34]
* **Work injury compensation.** Altostrat **fully complies with the Work Injury Compensation
  Act (WICA)**. If you sustain an injury **in the course of your employment**, the company
  will facilitate **medical coverage and compensation as mandated by the Ministry of Manpower
  (MOM)**.[^hb-34]

WICA compensation is a **statutory** scheme distinct from both
[health insurance](/people-ops/health-insurance.md) reimbursement and
[sick and hospitalisation leave](/leave/sick-and-hospitalisation.md). The handbook does not
explain how a work injury interacts with sick leave entitlement or MC requirements - if you
are injured at work, treat the interaction as unspecified and route it to People Ops.

# Ergonomics

Employees **working in the office or teleworking** are encouraged to use the **ergonomic
assessments** available through the Company Website, to promote healthy posture and prevent
repetitive strain injuries.[^hb-34]

# Safety at a remote or flexible location

Employees **must inform their managers of any health and safety risks** at their flexible
working location, and employee and manager **should discuss whether the arrangement needs to
change** to address them.[^hb-5-4]

This is the only stated bridge between the office WSH regime and remote work. The handbook
does not say whether WSHA duties or WICA coverage extend to a home workstation.
