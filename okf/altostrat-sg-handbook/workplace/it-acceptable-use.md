# Acceptable use of IT systems

* **Company property.** All electronic facilities - laptops, mobile devices, corporate
  networks and software - are **company property**, provided to help you perform your
  job.[^hb-33]
* **Unauthorised software.** Do **not** download or install unapproved software, illegal
  content or pirated media on company devices. All hardware and software requests must be
  routed through the **Company Website**.[^hb-33]
* **Corporate monitoring.** Altostrat **reserves the right to monitor, access and disclose**
  employee communications and file usage on corporate electronic facilities **where there is
  a business need** - for example protecting security, investigating misconduct, or ensuring
  compliance.[^hb-33]

This monitoring right applies **equally in the office, away from it, and fully remote** -
see [remote and hybrid work](/workplace/remote-and-hybrid-work.md).

# Social media

* **Protecting information.** Never disclose **confidential, proprietary or unreleased
  product information** on personal social media accounts or external platforms.[^hb-33]
* **Speaking for Altostrat.** Do not represent, or give the impression, that you speak on
  behalf of the company **unless explicitly authorised by Corporate
  Communications**.[^hb-33]

The [community guidelines](/conduct/community-guidelines.md) anti-trolling rule extends to
remarks about **public figures**, not only colleagues, so it constrains personal social
media conduct as well.

# ITSM ticket handling

These rules appear in the handbook under **Community Guidelines**, where they were
misfiled; they are IT service-management rules.

* **Ticket lifecycle.** All service tickets must progress **sequentially** through the
  standard operational states: **New → In Progress → Resolved → Closed**. **Bypassing
  intermediate states** - for example moving a ticket directly from New to Closed - **is a
  compliance violation and is strictly prohibited**.[^hb-5-5]
* **Priority definitions.** Ticket priority must **accurately reflect true business
  impact**. Minor facility issues, such as a squeaky office chair or cosmetic wear, must be
  classified **'4 - Low'**. Attempts to **artificially inflate priority** for non-emergencies
  **will be programmatically downgraded**.[^hb-5-5]

Only two priority levels are named anywhere in the handbook: **'3 - Moderate'** for
destination-office badging pre-configuration (see
[travel and expense](/workplace/travel-and-expense.md)) and **'4 - Low'** for minor facility
issues. The full priority scale is not defined in the source.

# Where ITSM is used across the handbook

ITSM is the stated channel for a wide range of unrelated requests. Collected here for
routing:

| Request | Category or type |
|---------|------------------|
| Hardware - monitors, security keys | **ServiceImmediately** case, with manager approval[^hb-5-5] |
| Remote equipment orders | **'Facilities'** category, shipped to verified remote address |
| Destination-office badging | **'Facilities'**, Priority **'3 - Moderate'** |
| Travel booking | **'Travel'** request - but see the conflict in [travel and expense](/workplace/travel-and-expense.md) |
| Flexible work request | **HR** case (or email to your manager) |
| Adjusting your remote schedule | Case in ITSM (or inform your manager) |
| Government business-courtesy pre-approval | RCI case - but see the conflict in [anti-bribery](/ethics/anti-bribery.md) |

Hardware requests are stated as going through **ITSM** in Sections 5.4 and 30, but Section
33.1 says all hardware and software requests are routed through the **Company Website**. The
handbook does not say whether the Company Website is the front door to ITSM or a separate
system. `Company Website` behaves throughout Sections 31-35 as an unresolved placeholder -
it is also given as the location of the benefits claims portal, ergonomic assessments,
perk details, incident reporting and HR escalation. Logged in the
[source defect register](/references/source-defects.md).
