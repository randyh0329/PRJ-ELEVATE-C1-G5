# Notice of termination

* **Providing notice.** If you plan to leave, you must give notice of termination **as
  required by your employment contract**.[^hb-29] The handbook states no notice period of its
  own - the contract governs.
* **Resigning while on leave.** If you are on an approved leave of absence - sick, maternity,
  baby bonding, carer's - and do **not** plan to return, you must **discuss it with your
  manager and contact support as soon as possible**, and you are **still required to give
  contractual notice**.[^hb-29]
* **End date.** All active **leave accruals and approved time-off periods stop immediately**
  on your official End Date.[^hb-29]

# Payout of leave balances

| Balance | Treatment on separation |
|---------|-------------------------|
| **Vacation leave** | **Paid out** - current year's unused balance, **prorated** for time worked in the year, **plus** any eligible carryover balance[^hb-29] |
| **Negative vacation balance** | **Deducted from your final paycheck** if you took more than you earned[^hb-29] |
| **Floating holidays** | **Not cashed out**[^hb-29] |
| **Sick leave** | Not paid out[^hb-29] |
| **Hospitalisation leave** | Not paid out[^hb-29] |
| **Childcare leave** | Not paid out[^hb-29] |
| **Maternity leave** | Not paid out[^hb-29] |
| **Baby bonding leave** | Not paid out[^hb-29] |
| **Carer's leave** | Not paid out[^hb-29] |

**Vacation is the only leave type that converts to money.** Carryover is capped at **one
year** and expires if unused[^hb-20-3] - see [vacation](/leave/vacation.md) for the accrual
tiers and the carryover rule that determines the payable balance.

Two leave types in this bundle are **absent from the payout list**: **bereavement leave**
and **time off in lieu (TOIL)**. Unpaid time off and personal leave, being unpaid, have no
balance to settle. TOIL in particular is earned time - the handbook does not say whether
unused TOIL is paid, forfeited, or must be consumed before the End Date. Do not answer
that from this bundle; route it to People Ops. Logged in the
[source defect register](/references/source-defects.md).

# Post-employment obligations

* **Continuing agreements.** After employment ends you remain subject to your **employment
  contract** and the **Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement** you
  signed on joining.[^hb-29]
* **Intellectual property.** Any IP you created **during your employment - including during
  any leave periods prior to separation** - belongs to the company, except as specifically
  excluded by your agreement.[^hb-29]
* **Confidential information.** **Properly secure, label and dispose of** confidential
  material **before your departure**, and **do not disclose** proprietary company information
  after you leave.[^hb-29]

The IP clause is explicit that leave does not create a gap in assignment - work produced
while on maternity, baby bonding or personal leave is still company IP.

# Retention of employee data

Your employment record is **kept as needed to protect the company from legal claims and to
satisfy legal and compliance obligations**. The **specific retention period depends on local
law** in the country of employment.[^hb-29]

No retention period is stated for Singapore. See
[employee privacy](/people-ops/employee-privacy.md) for the retention and rights framework.
