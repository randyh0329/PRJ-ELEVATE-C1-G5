# Frugality and booking

* Employees are expected to keep travel **frugal** and spend Altostrat's money
  wisely.[^hb-4]
* **Pre-travel checklist:** discuss the trip with your manager; note the **US$120 per day**
  meal cap; create a **Travel ticket request in ITSM**; **review the global risk map**
  before departing.[^hb-4]
* **Advance booking:** air and hotel bookings must be completed **at least 3 weeks in
  advance** to secure reasonable rates.[^hb-4]

# Company card and expense submission

| Rule | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| **Company Card mandate** | Required for travel spend if you incur **>US$10,000 per quarter** in business expenses, **or** any **single transaction over US$5,000**[^hb-4] |
| **Submission window** | All travel expenses through **Concur within 30 days** of the transaction or travel end date[^hb-4] |

**Aged out-of-pocket claims:**[^hb-4]

| Age of claim | Approval required |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Over 60 days | **Director** approval |
| Over 90 days | **VP** approval |
| Over one year | **Non-reimbursable** |

Approvals must come from **someone higher up the chain than your immediate
manager**.[^hb-4]

Note the two aged-claim bands overlap at their boundary in the same way the gift tiers do,
but less consequentially: a 95-day claim clears both the 60-day and 90-day rules, and the
higher approval (VP) evidently governs.

# Lodging and transportation

* **Caps:** flight and hotel choices are **strictly subject to regional route
  caps**.[^hb-4] The caps themselves are not stated in the handbook.
* **Staying with friends or family** in lieu of a hotel entitles you to a **host gift of up
  to US$50 per day**, backed by valid receipts. **Cash or gift-card host gifts are strictly
  prohibited.**[^hb-4]
* **Ground transport:** prioritise **public transit or shared rides**. Personal car use is
  reimbursable **per distance** in Concur - this **covers fuel**, and **separate fuel
  expenses are prohibited**. Car rentals must be **midsize/intermediate or
  smaller**.[^hb-4]

# Meals and entertainment

* **Daily meal limit:** **US$120 (or equivalent) per employee per day** for individual meals
  on global business trips. **This is not a per diem** - all meal expenses must be
  individually detailed with receipts.[^hb-4]
* **Group meals with Altostrat colleagues:** also capped at **US$120 per employee per
  day**. You must **list all attending colleagues in Concur** for tax compliance, and **the
  most senior colleague present must pay** and submit the expense, so that manager approval
  is independent.[^hb-4]
* **VP pre-approval:** any group meal or customer entertainment event costing **US$500 or
  greater per head** requires **written, pre-event VP approval attached to the Concur
  report**.[^hb-4]
* **Non-reimbursable:** room movies, traffic and parking fines, personal gifts.[^hb-4]

Customer and partner entertainment is additionally governed by the
[commercial gifts](/ethics/commercial-gifts.md) tiers and frequency limit, and - where a
government official is present - by [anti-bribery](/ethics/anti-bribery.md), which is
stricter and requires RCI pre-approval.

# Relocation to an international office

These two rules appear in the handbook under **Community Guidelines**, which is where they
were misfiled; they are substantively travel and facilities rules.

* **Relocation allowance:** employees transferring to international offices (for example the
  London HQ) are eligible for a relocation allowance **capped at US$10,000** to cover moving
  and transition expenses.[^hb-5-5]
* **Destination badging:** transferring employees must request **physical building badging
  pre-configuration for the destination office prior to arrival**, by opening a ticket with
  **Category 'Facilities', Priority '3 - Moderate'**.[^hb-5-5]

# Underlying expense-integrity duty

Any reimbursement claim must be **reasonable, directly related to company business, and
supported by appropriate documentation**, with the business purpose recorded accurately.
Falsifying an expense account is expressly prohibited.[^hb-6-3] See
[confidentiality and assets](/ethics/confidentiality-and-assets.md).

# Conflict

**Travel booking channel.** Section 4.1 says to **create a ticket request as Travel from
ITSM**.[^hb-4] Section 30's onboarding guide says all flights and hotels must be booked
**through the TRIPS portal**.[^hb-30-onboarding] Both state the 3-week advance rule, so the
timeline is not in doubt - only the system. The handbook does not reconcile them.

Do not answer "where do I book travel?" from this bundle alone; route it to People Ops or
the travel team. Logged in the [source defect register](/references/source-defects.md).
