# Hybrid structure

* The hybrid work week offers flexibility in assigned office locations. Employees may work
  **approximately three days a week in the office and two days away** (or nearby), **or
  work fully remote**.[^hb-5-4]
* These arrangements **may be considered telework** in your location of
  employment.[^hb-5-4]
* **Local compliance overrides.** Hybrid work is a global option, but **local mandatory
  requirements in certain locations override the global policy**.[^hb-5-4]

# Working time and availability

* **Regular hours.** While working away from the office, generally remain available during
  your regular work hours. To adjust your schedule you must **inform your manager or open a
  case in ITSM**.[^hb-5-4]
* **Statutory limits.** Applicable statutory work-hour limits and minimum break
  requirements for your **assigned office location** remain **exactly the same** during
  remote work.[^hb-5-4]

# Data protection guardrails

* **Public settings.** **Do not work on Altostrat confidential or proprietary projects in
  public settings** such as coffee shops or public libraries.[^hb-5-4]
* **Privacy measures.** When working on sensitive information away from the office you
  must:[^hb-5-4]
  * use a **privacy screen**;
  * **wear headphones** during virtual/GVC meetings;
  * keep **sensitive documents physically secured**.
* **Information security.** Protect confidential and proprietary company and customer
  information per the information security policies.[^hb-5-4]
* **Corporate monitoring.** The electronic monitoring and corporate systems policy applies
  **equally** whether you work from the office, away from it, or fully
  remote.[^hb-5-4]

# Equipment and allowances

* **Hardware** - monitors, security keys and similar - requires a **ServiceImmediately case
  in ITSM** with **manager approval**.[^hb-5-4]
* **Home office equipment allowance:** employees with an approved **'Remote' or 'Hybrid'**
  location status are eligible for a **US$500 allowance** for home office equipment,
  including monitors.[^hb-5-4]
* All remote equipment orders must be submitted via a **'Facilities' category ticket** and
  **shipped directly to the employee's verified remote shipping address**.[^hb-5-4]
* **Internet reimbursement:** employees may be eligible for home internet reimbursement,
  **subject to manager approval**. Some countries have additional statutory requirements for
  telework reimbursement.[^hb-5-4]

# Health and safety at the flexible location

Employees **must inform their managers of any health and safety risks** at their flexible
working location. Employees and managers should discuss whether the home working
arrangement needs to change to address them.[^hb-5-4]

See [workplace health and safety](/workplace/health-and-safety.md) for the ergonomics
assessment route.
