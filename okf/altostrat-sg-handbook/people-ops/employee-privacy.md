# Scope

* **Applies to** all employees, and to those working at **affiliates that do not have their
  own employee privacy policies**.[^hb-16]
* **Employee Data** means any information that **identifies an employee, or can be used to
  identify one, in the employment context** - **regardless of format, media or
  source**.[^hb-16]

# What is collected

| Category | Includes |
|----------|----------|
| **Recruitment** | Application and interview records, résumés/CVs, references, background check information[^hb-16] |
| **Personal details** | Contact information, **bank and direct deposit details**, **government identification numbers**, emergency contacts, family composition, demographic information[^hb-16] |
| **Performance, compensation and benefits** | Performance ratings and assessments, equity awards or stock, pension documents, payroll details, **vacation records**, **working time records**[^hb-16] |
| **Working data** | Survey data, **disciplinary and grievance records**, **security recordings and badging records**, **logs and usage data of company equipment, accounts and systems**[^hb-16] |

The last row is the collection basis for the monitoring right asserted in
[IT acceptable use](/workplace/it-acceptable-use.md), and it covers badging records, which
[onboarding](/workplace/onboarding.md) requires you to generate daily.

# How it is used

* **Business and legal purposes:** recruitment, compensation and payroll, performance
  assessment, talent management, surveys, legal compliance and workplace management; also to
  **protect the company and workforce against injury, theft or liability**, to **identify and
  investigate policy violations**, to administer background checks, and to **make employment
  decisions**.[^hb-16]
* **Technology development:** the company **may use Employee Data to help develop, train,
  fine-tune and improve machine learning models and foundational technologies** powering
  products and internal tools, **where it is in the company's legitimate interests to do
  so**.[^hb-16]
* **Sensitive data** - health, racial or ethnic origins, sexual orientation - is processed
  **only in limited circumstances**: when necessary for **legal claims**, **with your
  consent**, or where **required by local employment and social security law**. The stated
  examples are using medical information to administer sick pay, and sexual orientation data
  to administer dependent benefits.[^hb-16]

Two things follow that are worth stating plainly. The **ML training use is opt-out-free as
written** - it rests on legitimate interest, and the handbook offers no mechanism to decline
it beyond the general right to object in §16.5. And the **sick-pay example is live**: the
MCs you submit under [sick and hospitalisation leave](/leave/sick-and-hospitalisation.md)
are sensitive data processed on the legal-requirement basis.

# Security and sharing

* **Internal access** is restricted to entities and employees who **need it to carry out
  their assigned employment-related functions**.[^hb-16]
* **Employee communication rights are preserved.** Nothing in the policy limits an
  employee's rights, in a personal capacity, to **share contact information or otherwise
  communicate about pay, hours, or other terms and conditions of work** with fellow employees
  or **government agencies**.[^hb-16]
* **External disclosures** may be made for business and legal purposes - to insurers, legal
  advisers, payroll providers, background check agencies, or **government agencies for
  mandatory reporting**.[^hb-16]
* **Third parties** - **including temps, vendors and contractors** - processing Employee Data
  on the company's behalf must use it **only as directed**, protect it per applicable data
  protection regulation, and **refrain from unauthorised disclosure**.[^hb-16]

# Retention and your rights

* **Retention:** data is kept **as long as needed** for the purposes above. Employment
  records are generally maintained **for the period of employment**, and afterwards **as
  needed to protect against legal claims and satisfy compliance obligations**, which **may
  depend on local law**.[^hb-16][^hb-29-4] No Singapore-specific period is stated anywhere in
  the handbook.
* **Employee rights**, *depending on the country*: request **access, correction, amendment,
  anonymisation or deletion** of certain Employee Data, and **object to or restrict**
  processing.[^hb-16] The handbook does **not** say which of these apply in Singapore.
* **Access exceptions:** confidential or proprietary data such as **talent planning data**;
  where disclosure would **violate others' privacy rights**; **ongoing investigations of
  malfeasance**; or where disclosure would **prejudice the company's interests in
  litigation**.[^hb-16]

The malfeasance-investigation exception means an employee under investigation under
[performance and discipline](/conduct/performance-and-discipline.md) or
[harassment and discrimination](/conduct/harassment-and-discrimination.md) may be refused
access to the data being used, while the investigation is ongoing.

# International transfers

* Because the company operates globally, Employee Data **may be processed outside your
  country or region, including in the United States**.[^hb-16]
* Transfer mechanisms relied on: **European Commission / UK / Swiss Adequacy Decisions**, the
  **EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Frameworks (DPF)**, and **Standard Contractual
  Clauses (SCCs)**.[^hb-16]

Note that these are all **EU/UK/Swiss** instruments. This is a **Singapore** handbook, and
none of the listed mechanisms addresses a transfer **out of Singapore** under the PDPA. The
source does not name a Singapore transfer basis.

# Inquiries and complaints

* **General employment questions:** contact **HR**.[^hb-16]
* **To exercise privacy rights or complain:** your **Manager**, **HR**, or **open a case in
  ITSM**.[^hb-16]
* **Data Protection Officer:** given in the source as **`abc@altostrat.com`** - an
  **unresolved placeholder**, not a working address. Logged in the
