# Payroll and salary

* **Pay periods.** Salaries are paid **monthly**, by **direct deposit** to the employee's
  designated bank account, **by the last working day of the month**.[^hb-31]
* **Deductions.** The company makes all necessary **statutory deductions**, including
  **income tax withholdings for foreign workers** and **statutory provident fund
  contributions**.[^hb-31]

# Central Provident Fund (CPF)

For **Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents**, Altostrat contributes to the **Central
Provident Fund** in strict accordance with the **prevailing rates set by the CPF Board**.
**Both the employer's and the employee's share** are processed **automatically through
payroll**.[^hb-31]

The handbook deliberately does **not** state CPF rates - it defers to the CPF Board's
prevailing rates, which vary by age band and wage ceiling and change over time. **Do not
quote a CPF rate from this bundle.** There is none to quote, and any figure would be
invented. Route rate questions to the CPF Board or payroll.

Nothing is stated about CPF treatment for **Employment Pass holders and other foreign
workers** beyond the income-tax withholding note; CPF is not payable for them under
Singapore law, but the handbook does not say so.

# Non-leave perks and benefits

Employees **may be eligible** for:[^hb-31]

* Monthly **internet reimbursements**
* **Wellness / gym subsidies**
* **Mobile phone allowances**

Details and eligibility are stated to be on the **Company Website** - an unresolved
placeholder throughout Sections 31-35. No amounts, eligibility criteria or claim routes are
given for any of the three.

**Home internet is stated twice, differently framed.** Section 31.3 lists "monthly internet
reimbursements" as a perk; Section 5.4 says employees **may be eligible for home internet
reimbursement, subject to manager approval**, with additional statutory requirements in some
countries.[^hb-5-4] The two are compatible - manager approval is the gate - but only §5.4
states the gate.

The **US$500 home office equipment allowance** for approved Remote or Hybrid employees is
not listed here; it sits in [remote and hybrid work](/workplace/remote-and-hybrid-work.md).
The **US$10,000 international relocation allowance** sits in
[travel and expense](/workplace/travel-and-expense.md).

# What this section does not cover

The handbook contains no statement of:

* **Salary review or increment** timing or basis - the annual cycle in
  [performance and discipline](/conduct/performance-and-discipline.md) evaluates goals and
  competencies but is not linked to pay.
* **Bonus or variable compensation** structure.
* **Equity or stock** mechanics. Equity awards appear only as a *data category* in
  [employee privacy](/people-ops/employee-privacy.md).
* **Overtime pay.** Extra hours are handled as
  [time off in lieu](/leave/time-off-in-lieu.md), not as payment.
* **Payslip access** or dispute route.
