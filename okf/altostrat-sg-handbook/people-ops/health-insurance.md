# Coverage

Altostrat provides comprehensive health insurance for **all eligible full-time and part-time
employees**. Coverage includes:[^hb-32]

* **Inpatient hospitalisation**
* **Outpatient** general practitioner (GP) and specialist care
* **Dental**
* **Vision**

The handbook states no **coverage limits, sub-limits, co-payment, deductible or excluded
condition** of any kind, and does not name the insurer. It also does not define "eligible"
beyond the full-time/part-time split, and does not say whether **dependants** are covered -
though [employee privacy](/people-ops/employee-privacy.md) refers to using sexual orientation
data "to administer dependent benefits", which implies dependant coverage exists somewhere.

**Extended workforce** - temps, vendors, contractors - are not mentioned; the wording says
*employees*.

# Claims process

To be reimbursed for out-of-pocket medical, dental or vision expenses covered by the
plan:[^hb-32]

1. **Obtain documentation.** An **itemised final receipt** *and* a **medical certificate or
   diagnosis note** from the attending physician or clinic.
2. **Submit via portal.** Log into the **benefits claims portal** on the Company Website.
3. **Upload details.** Select the claim category (Outpatient GP, Dental, …), enter
   **treatment date, diagnosis and total amount paid**, and upload **clear, legible copies**
   of receipts and medical documents.
4. **Timelines.** All medical claims must be submitted **within 30 days of the treatment
   date**. **Late submissions may be rejected by the insurance provider.**
5. **Reimbursement.** Once approved by the insurance provider, reimbursement is **credited
   directly to your bank account alongside standard monthly payroll**.

Note step 1: **every** claim requires a diagnosis note, not just a receipt - including
routine dental or vision. And the approval decision rests with **the insurance provider**,
not Altostrat, so there is no internal appeal route stated.

# Two distinct 30-day clocks, two distinct MCs

Do not conflate these:

| | Health insurance claim | Sick leave |
|---|---|---|
| **Deadline** | **30 days** from the **treatment date**[^hb-32] | MC **within 48 hours** of a sickness exceeding two consecutive work days |
| **Document** | Itemised receipt **+** diagnosis note | **Medical Certificate** from a registered practitioner |
| **Submitted to** | Benefits claims portal (Company Website) | WorkWeek |
| **Purpose** | Money back for out-of-pocket cost | Justify paid absence |

A single illness can trigger both. See
[sick and hospitalisation leave](/leave/sick-and-hospitalisation.md).

# Not health insurance

* **Work injuries** are compensated under **WICA**, a statutory scheme administered through
  MOM - see [workplace health and safety](/workplace/health-and-safety.md). The handbook does
  not say whether you claim insurance, WICA, or both for a workplace injury.
* **Hospitalisation *leave*** is a leave entitlement (46 work days), not a benefit payment -
  see [sick and hospitalisation leave](/leave/sick-and-hospitalisation.md).
