# Definition

Employees and extended workforce members are expected to **do what is best for
Altostrat**.[^hb-12]

A **conflict of interest** arises when competing loyalties could cause an employee to
pursue a benefit for themselves - or for individuals with whom they have a **personal,
financial or close friend relationship** - at the expense of the company or its
users.[^hb-12]

**A conflict, or even the appearance of one**, may impair innovation, risk disclosure of
confidential information, taint intellectual property, or undermine public and user trust
in Altostrat's integrity.[^hb-12]

**Consequences:** discipline up to and including termination of employment, or of job
assignment for extended workforce members.[^hb-12]

# Obligations

**Employees** must:[^hb-12]

1. **Avoid** circumstances that create a conflict or the appearance of one.
2. If subject to - **or considering entering into** - such circumstances, **disclose to
   your manager** and **seek review from Ethics and Business Integrity (EBI)**.
3. **Comply with any restrictions or mitigations** EBI sets out.

Note the forward-looking trigger: the duty attaches when you are *considering* the
arrangement, not only once you are in it.

**Managers** who receive a disclosure, or become aware of circumstances that may create a
conflict or its appearance, must **seek guidance from VP**.[^hb-12]

# Enumerated conflict categories

The following must be disclosed:[^hb-12]

| # | Category | Detail |
|---|----------|--------|
| 1 | **Outside work** | Work involving a competitor or a competing product/service, or that overlaps with and impairs your current role |
| 2 | **Vendor and supplier relationships** | A personal, financial or close friend relationship with a vendor/supplier or a person there, when you are involved in their selection or management, or otherwise work with them |
| 3 | **Internal relationships** | A personal relationship with an employee, extended workforce member or active candidate over whom you exercise supervision or authority - see [personal relationships](/conduct/personal-relationships.md) |
| 4 | **External relationships** | A personal, financial or close friend relationship with an external customer or partner over whom you have influence in your job |
| 5 | **Investments** | Making or holding a **significant investment** in a competitor, or in a company you work with or influence. Trading securities while holding material nonpublic information falls under the separate Insider Trading Policy |
| 6 | **Corporate resources** | Using corporate resources for personal financial gain or a non-company business, or personally profiting from transactions based on your relationship with Altostrat |
| 7 | **Internal access** | Using internal tools, products or confidential information to improperly benefit yourself or someone you know, or to create an unfair advantage over outside users |
| 8 | **Gifts and entertainment** | Accepting personal gifts or entertainment from customers or suppliers, except in strict compliance with the [anti-bribery](/ethics/anti-bribery.md) and [gifts](/ethics/commercial-gifts.md) policies |
| 9 | **Business opportunities** | Exploiting business opportunities discovered through your work for a non-company purpose, unless otherwise agreed |
| 10 | **Former government employees** | May be subject to special conflict-of-interest limitations on what they can do at Altostrat |

# EBI review

On being notified of a potential conflict, EBI consults relevant stakeholders
to:[^hb-12]

* **Evaluate** the circumstances and determine the risk of a conflict or its appearance.
* **Recommend mitigations** - which may include ceasing the conflicting activity,
  implementing an **internal recusal**, or other measures.

# Overlap with outside employment rules

Section 6.4 states related obligations that are not repeated in Section 12:[^hb-6-4]

* Avoid employment, advisory positions or **board seats** with competitors or business
  partners if your judgement could be influenced in a way that harms Altostrat.
* **You must notify your manager before accepting a board seat with any outside company** -
  not only a competitor.
* Do not start a business that will compete with Altostrat.
* **Business opportunities discovered through your work belong first to Altostrat.**

See [confidentiality and assets](/ethics/confidentiality-and-assets.md), where Section 6.4
is carried in full.

# Conflicts

**1. Manager escalation path.** Section 12.2 tells employees to seek review from **EBI**,
but tells managers to seek guidance from **VP**.[^hb-12] The same disclosure therefore
routes to two different destinations depending on who is holding it, and the policy does
not say whether the manager's VP consultation replaces or precedes EBI review.

**2. EBI contact channel.** Section 12.2 names EBI without a channel.[^hb-12] The
onboarding section gives the channel as the literal placeholder `company
intranet`.[^hb-30-onboarding] There is no resolved contact route in the source.

Both are logged in the [source defect register](/references/source-defects.md).
