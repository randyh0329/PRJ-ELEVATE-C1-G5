# The rule

**Don't bribe.** Never offer, give or receive bribes in connection with company work, to
anyone, at any time, for any reason.[^hb-13]

Altostrat, its officers, employees, and third parties acting on its behalf cannot
**directly or indirectly** give, offer or promise anything of value to a government
official with **corrupt intent to obtain or retain an improper advantage**.[^hb-13]

**Facilitation payments.** "Facilitation" or "grease" payments to expedite routine,
non-discretionary governmental actions - permits, visas, licences - are **strictly
prohibited regardless of amount**.[^hb-13]

**Consequences.** Civil and criminal penalties for both the company and the individuals
involved, plus discipline up to and including termination.[^hb-13]

# Definitions

**"Something of value"** includes:[^hb-13]

* Cash or cash equivalents - gift cards, certificates, virtual money
* Gifts and entertainment
* Meals
* Travel and hotel
* **Offers of employment, including internships** (the summary layer specifies
  **unpaid** internships)[^hb-5-1]
* Charitable and political contributions
* Anything else of tangible or intangible value

**"Government official"** includes:[^hb-13]

* Employees of national, state and local governments
* Employees of **government-owned or -controlled businesses** - public universities,
  national telecom companies
* Employees of **public international organisations** - UN, World Bank
* Political parties and officials
* Candidates for public office
* **Members of royal families**

# Pre-approval requirements

A business courtesy or modest gift to a government official **can** be permissible where
it has a legitimate business purpose and is given transparently and infrequently. The
following require **written pre-approval from the Risk, Compliance & Integrity (RCI)
team**:[^hb-13]

| Situation | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| **U.S. government officials** | **Anything** of value, **regardless of amount** - the sole exception being non-alcoholic drinks at a meeting hosted at an Altostrat office |
| **Non-U.S. government officials** | Value **exceeding US$100**, **or** cumulative courtesies to that official **exceeding US$200 within a rolling 6-month period** |
| **Government entities** | A contribution, donation or gift of products, services or money **of any value** - for example devices to a Ministry of Education |
| **Political contributions** | Any **corporate** political contribution, monetary or in-kind, to any political organisation, party, candidate or committee. Does not apply to purely personal contributions unless RCI has given specific guidance |
| **Hosting events** | Before hosting or sponsoring an event where you have reason to believe government officials will receive anything of value that would otherwise require pre-approval |
| **Receiving value** | Before **receiving** anything of value **exceeding US$100** from any government entity, official or political party |

Note the last row: the policy is **bidirectional**. Receiving is controlled, not just
giving.

# Prohibited outright

Business courtesies and gifts cannot:[^hb-13]

* Violate any law or regulation
* Be intended, or reasonably perceived as an attempt, to improperly influence official
  action
* Embarrass or reflect negatively on Altostrat's reputation - **no adult entertainment,
  no gambling**
* Be cash or a cash equivalent

# Third-party intermediaries

**Altostrat can be held criminally liable for the misconduct of its business
partners.**[^hb-13]

All employees are responsible for ensuring **RCI due diligence** is completed for every
intermediary - business partners, consultants, resellers - who will interact with
government officials on Altostrat's behalf. Due diligence is initiated at **ITSM** and
**must be completed before the third party begins working**.[^hb-13]

# Record-keeping

* Record all transactions in the books with **a reasonable level of specificity**. Never
  conceal the nature of a business courtesy using vague categories such as
  **"marketing"**.[^hb-13]
* On a PO or expense request, **list the government official's name** and check **"yes"**
  to the **"government-related"** flag.[^hb-13]
* The summary layer states the same requirement as "absolute specificity".[^hb-5-1]

# Reporting

If you become aware of a bribe being offered or requested, report it **immediately** to
RCI via a case in ITSM, or to the **anonymous helpline** on the intranet.[^hb-13]
