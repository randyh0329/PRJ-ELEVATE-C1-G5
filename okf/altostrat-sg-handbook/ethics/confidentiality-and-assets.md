# Preserving confidentiality

* **Preventing leaks.** Never prematurely reveal confidential company information to the
  press or competitors - it can harm product launches and eliminate competitive
  advantage.[^hb-6]
* **Information handling.** Properly **secure, label and dispose of** confidential
  material. Anything classified **"Need to Know"** or **"Confidential"** must be handled
  strictly per data security guidelines.[^hb-6]
* **Third-party disclosure.** Only on an **"only as needed"** basis and **strictly under a
  formally approved NDA**. You must also safeguard confidential information Altostrat
  receives from partners under an NDA.[^hb-6]
* **Former employers.** Do not take advantage of a competitor's or former employer's
  confidential information.[^hb-6]

# Company assets and intellectual property

* **IP rights.** Trademarks, logos, copyrights, trade secrets and patents are among
  Altostrat's most valuable assets; unauthorised use can cause serious loss of value.
  **Never use company logos, marks or protected property for any outside business or
  commercial venture without pre-clearance from Marketing.**[^hb-6]
* **Equipment.** Company funds, equipment and physical assets are provided to do your job
  and **must not be requisitioned for purely personal use**.[^hb-6]
* **Physical security.** Always secure your laptop and personal belongings, **even on
  company premises**. Wear your badge visibly while on site. **Never tamper with or disable
  security and safety devices.**[^hb-6]

# Financial integrity and contracting

* **Spending company money.** Any reimbursement claim must be **reasonable, directly
  related to company business, and supported by appropriate documentation**. Always record
  the business purpose accurately.[^hb-6]
* **Signing contracts.** A business transaction requires written documentation **approved
  by the Legal Department**. Never sign a contract unless you are **officially authorised**,
  Legal has approved it, and **you have read and understood its terms**.[^hb-6]
* **Side agreements prohibited.** All contracts must be in writing and contain all
  relevant terms. Altostrat **strictly prohibits any oral or written "side
  agreements"**.[^hb-6]
* **Audits and records.** Never interfere with the auditing of financial records or
  falsify any record - **including time reports and expense accounts**.[^hb-6]

# Outside employment and business ventures

* **Board seats and advisory roles.** Avoid employment, advisory positions or board seats
  with competitors or business partners if your judgement could be influenced in a way that
  harms Altostrat. **You must notify your manager before accepting a board seat with any
  outside company** - the notification duty is not limited to competitors.[^hb-6]
* **Starting a business.** Do not start your own business if it will **compete** with
  Altostrat.[^hb-6]
* **Business opportunities.** Any business opportunity discovered through your work
  **belongs first to Altostrat**.[^hb-6]
