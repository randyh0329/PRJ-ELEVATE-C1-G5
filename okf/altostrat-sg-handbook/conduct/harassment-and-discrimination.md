# Scope

Altostrat commits to a work environment free from sexual harassment, discrimination,
misconduct, abusive conduct and retaliation.[^hb-7]

* **Who is covered:** applicants, employees, extended workforce members (temps, vendors,
  contractors), customers and visitors.[^hb-7]
* **Where it applies:** at work, at work-related social events such as off-sites and
  office parties, and on corporate networks and systems.[^hb-7]
* **Consequences:** discipline up to and including termination for employees. For
  extended workforce members and third parties, removal from the premises or termination
  of their business contracts.[^hb-7]

# Harassment

Unwelcome conduct based on a protected status that creates an intimidating, hostile or
abusive environment. Includes derogatory jokes, slurs, non-verbal staring or gestures,
sharing offensive images, and unwanted bodily contact.[^hb-7]

# Sexual harassment

Unwelcome conduct of a sexual nature. Prohibited behaviours include unwanted sexual
advances, **quid pro quo** demands - making submission to conduct a condition of
employment - displaying sexually explicit images, and unwanted physical contact such as
groping.[^hb-7]

**No excuses.** Sexual harassment can occur regardless of the gender, gender identity or
orientation of those involved. "It was just a joke" and "there was alcohol involved" are
**not acceptable excuses**.[^hb-7]

# Discrimination

Treating someone less favourably because of a protected status is strictly prohibited.
This includes adverse actions such as **intentionally reducing someone's performance
score** or **denying a promotion** on those grounds.[^hb-7]

**Protected statuses:** race, colour, religion, veteran status, national origin,
ancestry, pregnancy status, sex, gender identity or expression, age, marital status,
mental or physical disability, medical condition, and sexual orientation.[^hb-7]

# Bullying and other misconduct

* **Bullying:** repeated - or seriously one-off - offensive, intimidating or insulting
  behaviour. Includes outbursts of aggression, spreading malicious rumours, and repeatedly
  shouting at or humiliating a colleague in front of others.[^hb-7]
* **Doxxing:** disclosing a coworker's personal information - residential address,
  personal cell number, financial information - without consent, to subject them to abuse
  or harassment.[^hb-7]
* **Also prohibited:** violent threats, theft, insubordination, unethical behaviour such
  as falsifying records, accessing data without permission, possession of weapons, and
  excessive absenteeism - defined as **three or more consecutive days absent without
  notice**.[^hb-7]

# Retaliation

Strictly prohibited against anyone who raises a concern, participates in an
investigation, or assists another employee in reporting a policy violation.[^hb-7]

# Reporting

Employees may raise concerns through:[^hb-7]

* Their manager
* Human Resources
* The **Respect@** team
* An **anonymous report through the Compliance Helpline**

Concerns involving an executive - the CEO, a direct report to the CEO, or a Senior VP -
may be escalated to the **Audit Committee of the Board of Directors**.[^hb-7]

# Manager obligations

Managers must foster a safe environment and **promptly report** any known or suspected
violation to HR, the Respect@ team or the Compliance Helpline. **Failure by a manager to
forward a complaint may itself result in discipline up to and including
termination.**[^hb-7]

# Investigations

All employees must cooperate and provide truthful information. **Providing false or
misleading information is itself a policy violation.** Altostrat treats inquiries as
confidentially as possible, disclosing on a need-to-know basis only, but **absolute
confidentiality cannot be guaranteed** - and the handbook says so explicitly rather than
implying it.[^hb-7]
