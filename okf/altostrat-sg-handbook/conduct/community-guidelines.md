# Principles

Community guidelines support healthy, open discussion while keeping the workplace focused
on the shared mission.[^hb-9]

* **Be responsible.** You are responsible for your words and actions and will be held
  accountable for them.[^hb-9]
* **Be helpful.** Your voice is your contribution; make it productive.[^hb-9]
* **Be thoughtful.** Your statements can be **attributed to the company regardless of
  your intent**, so avoid causing others to make incorrect assumptions.[^hb-9]
* **Company values.** All communication must follow the three core values: respect the
  user, respect the opportunity, respect each other.[^hb-9]

# Disruptive debate

* Your **primary responsibility is the work you were hired to do**, not debating non-work
  topics on working time.[^hb-9]
* Sharing ideas builds community, but **disrupting the WorkWeek for a raging debate over
  politics or the latest news story is prohibited**.[^hb-9]
* **Managers are fully expected to address** discussions that violate these
  rules.[^hb-9]

# Respect and anti-trolling

* Do not **troll, name-call, or engage in ad hominem attacks** against
  anyone.[^hb-9]
* This includes statements that **insult, demean or humiliate** other employees, extended
  workforce members, business partners, or **public figures**.[^hb-5-5]

# Accountability and data care

* **Debating company activities is permitted.** You are free to respectfully raise
  concerns, question and debate Altostrat's activities - that is part of the
  culture.[^hb-9]
* But you must **speak with good information**, avoid assuming you have the full story,
  and **not make false or misleading statements** about products or the business that could
  undermine trust.[^hb-9]
* Because workplace communications spread rapidly and broadly, you must **never access,
  disclose or disseminate "Need-to-Know" or "Confidential" information** in violation of
  the Data Security Policy.[^hb-9]

# Reporting violations

Three options:[^hb-9]

1. **Speak up in the moment**, if you feel comfortable.
2. **Report the content** by opening a case in ITSM, or reach the **group moderator** on
   internal platforms.
3. **Talk to an Advisor** to discuss and explore options.
