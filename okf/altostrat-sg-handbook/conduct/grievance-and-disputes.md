# Open door

Altostrat encourages open, honest communication. Workplace conflicts, misunderstandings
and operational disputes should be resolved **as close to the source as
possible**.[^hb-35]

# Formal grievance process

Where a concern cannot be resolved informally:[^hb-35]

| Step | Who | What | Timeline |
|------|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | **Direct manager** | Raise the grievance **in writing**, detailing the issue and the desired resolution | Manager reviews and responds **within 5 working days** |
| 2 | **HR Business Partner** | Escalate if the manager is involved in the grievance, or if the resolution is unsatisfactory. Route via the Company Website | Not stated |
| 3 | **Formal review** | HR conducts an impartial review, which may involve gathering statements and holding meetings with relevant parties | Not stated |

**Step 3 produces a final, binding resolution.**[^hb-35]

# Non-retaliation

No employee will face adverse action, demotion or retaliation for raising a grievance **in
good faith**, or for participating in a dispute resolution process.[^hb-35]

# Choosing this route versus the conduct route

The handbook maintains two separate escalation systems and does not explain how to choose
between them:

| Route | Use for | Channels |
|-------|---------|----------|
| **Grievance** (this concept) | Workplace conflict, misunderstanding, operational dispute | Manager, then HR Business Partner |
| **[Harassment and discrimination](/conduct/harassment-and-discrimination.md)** | Harassment, discrimination, misconduct, abusive conduct, retaliation | Manager, HR, **Respect@**, **Compliance Helpline** (anonymous), Audit Committee for executives |

The conduct route is the only one offering an **anonymous** channel and the only one with
an executive escalation path. A grievance about a manager's conduct plausibly falls under
both. The source does not say which governs.

# Gaps in the source

* No timeline is given for Step 2 or Step 3.
* "Final, binding" is stated without any appeal mechanism.
* The route for grievances raised by **extended workforce members** is not specified;
  Section 35 refers only to "employees".
