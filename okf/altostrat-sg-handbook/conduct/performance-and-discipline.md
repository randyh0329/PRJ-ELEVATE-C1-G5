# Performance management

* **Annual cycles.** Employees participate in annual Performance Management cycles to
  evaluate goals, assess core competencies and discuss career growth.[^hb-30-perf]
* **Ongoing feedback.** Managers and employees are expected to hold **regular 1:1
  check-ins** to stay aligned on business priorities and performance expectations
  throughout the year.[^hb-30-perf]

The rating scale is referenced elsewhere in the handbook as **GRAD**, with
"Significant Impact" named as a rating tier - see
[unpaid and personal leave](/leave/unpaid-and-personal.md) and
[vacation](/leave/vacation.md). The full scale is not defined anywhere in the source.

# Performance Improvement Plans

Where performance falls below expectations, the manager and HR **may** place the employee
on a formal **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)**. The PIP outlines **specific,
measurable goals and a timeline** for expected improvement. **Failure to meet PIP
objectives may result in reassignment or termination.**[^hb-30-perf]

# Progressive discipline

For violations of company policies or codes of conduct, Altostrat **generally** follows a
progressive process, but **reserves the right to accelerate it based on the severity of
the offence**.[^hb-30-perf]

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Verbal warning** - a documented conversation outlining the issue and the expected correction |
| 2 | **Written warning** - a formal notice placed in the personnel file |
| 3 | **Final written warning / suspension** - a final notice that continued behaviour means immediate termination, potentially with unpaid suspension |
| 4 | **Termination** - for repeated or severe violations, for example gross misconduct, fraud, violence or severe harassment |

Note that step 1 is described as "verbal" but is also "documented" - the record exists
even at the first step.
