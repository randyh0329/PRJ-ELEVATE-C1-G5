# General

Violating the expectations in this policy is subject to discipline **up to and including
termination**.[^hb-10]

# Alcohol

* **Not banned.** Alcohol consumption is not prohibited at Altostrat.[^hb-10]
* **But conduct standards do not move.** You are held to **exactly the same standards of
  conduct** whether or not alcohol is present. **Alcohol is never an excuse for
  inappropriate conduct.**[^hb-10]
* **Excessive consumption**, and **underage consumption**, are strictly prohibited at
  work, while performing business, or at a related event - onsite or offsite.[^hb-10]
* **Manager responsibilities:** people managers must ensure company-sponsored events,
  onsite and offsite, are safe. They must **avoid encouraging overconsumption** and are
  expected to address inappropriate conduct. **HR must respond** on becoming aware of
  excessive drinking or violations.[^hb-10]

# Smoking and vaping

* **Prohibited in any part of the office at any time.** This covers inside buildings,
  within posted distances outside entrances, and on **company shuttles**.[^hb-10]
* The ban includes **devices that resemble and operate like a cigarette** - cigars,
  e-cigarettes and other vapour smoke devices.[^hb-10]

# Illegal drugs and controlled substances

* Consuming, possessing, manufacturing, distributing or dispensing illegal controlled
  substances in offices, facilities or at work events is **strictly prohibited globally at
  all times**.[^hb-10]
* **Marijuana:** despite local or state laws, marijuana is a US DEA **Schedule I**
  controlled substance, so consumption is strictly prohibited at work, while performing
  company business, or at company-related events.[^hb-10]
* **Convictions:** you must notify Altostrat of any **criminal drug statute conviction for
  a violation occurring in the workplace within five days** of the conviction. On such a
  conviction, Altostrat will discipline you and/or require participation in a drug abuse or
  rehabilitation programme.[^hb-10]

# Prescription medication

* Lawful use and possession of over-the-counter or prescribed medication is **permitted**,
  provided it is used **by the intended individual** and **only as prescribed**.[^hb-10]
* **Safety disclosure:** consult your doctor about a medication's effect on fitness for
  duty and ability to work safely, and **promptly disclose any work restrictions** to your
  manager and the accommodations team.[^hb-10]

# Screenings

Where there is **reasonable suspicion** that an employee is under the influence in a
manner that could adversely affect job performance or safety, Altostrat may request a drug
and/or alcohol screening **as allowed under local law**.[^hb-10]

# Reporting and support

* Speak up through the channels in the Workplace Concern Policy if you observe
  inappropriate behaviour.[^hb-10]
* **Addiction is treated as an illness.** Altostrat provides referrals to drug counselling
  services, rehabilitation programmes, and employee assistance programmes
  (EAP).[^hb-10]
